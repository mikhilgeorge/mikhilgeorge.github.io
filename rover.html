Rover Central Housing Unit

A modular aluminum housing integrating rover electronics, computing, power distribution, and wiring.

Mechanical designCenter-of-mass analysisLeadership

Objective

Improve durability, subsystem accessibility, and interchangeability between arm and science payloads.

My contribution

Led the central housing redesign and used center-of-mass analysis to guide placement relative to the suspension.

Design decisions

When subsequent component changes introduced new requirements, evaluated rebuilding versus adapting the existing housing. Helped select targeted modifications based on schedule constraints and expected benefits, working collaboratively across the integrated system.

Skills used and developed

Mechanical integration, center-of-mass analysis, modular design, and collaborative decision-making.

The project involved managing subsystem interfaces and adapting an existing design when component requirements changed.
