Frunk Seal Insertion and Extraction Analysis

Used finite element analysis to compare flange thickness alternatives and support a sealing design recommendation.

FEAInsertion and extraction forcesTechnical reporting

Objective

Determine an optimal flange thickness for a frunk sealing application by comparing the forces associated with insertion and extraction.

My contribution

Compared peak insertion and extraction forces across 0.95, 1.50, and 2.75 mm flange thicknesses using FEA and documented recommendations in a technical report.

Analysis approach

Evaluated three discrete flange thicknesses and compared their peak force responses. The analysis supported a recommendation based on the insertion and extraction behavior of the sealing system.

Results

Delivered a thickness recommendation and a technical report documenting the comparison. The selected thickness and numerical force results are not included here.

Skills used and developed

Finite element analysis, comparison of design alternatives, force-response interpretation, and communication of engineering recommendations.
