Mechanism design

Servo-Controlled Marble Maze

A functioning two-axis servo-controlled maze developed from concept selection through prototype validation.

SolidWorksMotion studiesServo actuation
Project photos and CAD images will be added here.

Objective

Design and fabricate a two-axis mechanism for tilting a marble maze.

Architecture selection

Compared actuation architectures against rotational range, friction, and torque constraints before selecting a nested gimbal configuration.

My contribution and testing

Led design and fabrication, developed SolidWorks models and motion studies, integrated servo and shaft hardware, and refined mechanical interfaces through testing and redesign. Observed performance guided decisions about mechanism design and integration.

Skills used and developed

SolidWorks, motion studies, mechanism selection, servo and shaft integration, and prototype refinement.

The project involved connecting actuation constraints with mechanical architecture and using observed performance to refine interfaces.
