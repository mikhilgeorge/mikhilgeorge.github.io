Sensor packaging · Validation

Air Quality Sensor System

A servo-actuated outdoor sensor enclosure validated experimentally, with print mass reduced to 83.1 g.

CADExperimental validationMaterial efficiency

Objective

Integrate a particulate matter sensor, microcontroller, and detachable enclosure for outdoor environmental monitoring.

My contribution

Led CAD development and refined door tracks, component packaging, attachment hooks, and internal interfaces through successive prototypes.

Testing and results

Balanced airflow requirements with durability, manufacturability, and ease of assembly. Validated airflow and functionality experimentally, achieving readings comparable to outdoor control data while reducing print mass to 83.1 g. Used test results to guide improvements in sensing performance and material efficiency.
