Other Projects

Air Quality Sensor System

Designed and prototyped a servo-actuated outdoor air quality sensor housing integrating a PM sensor, microcontroller, and detachable enclosure. Led CAD development and refined component packaging, door tracks, and attachment interfaces through testing, achieving readings comparable to outdoor control data while reducing printed housing mass to 83.1 g.

Swivel Multi-bit Screwdriver

Designed and prototyped a compact multi-bit screwdriver, translating customer needs into engineering requirements and CAD models. Used House of Quality analysis to compare compatibility, weight, manufacturability, and accessibility, then refined prototypes to reduce print volume and improve usability across multiple fastener interfaces.

Bluetooth-Controlled Smart Car

Designed and built a Bluetooth-controlled robotic vehicle integrating Arduino programming, drivetrain hardware, and wireless communication. Developed control logic to translate wireless commands into motor actuation, then used iterative software and hardware testing to troubleshoot responses and improve vehicle functionality and reliability.

Ergonomic Portable Desk

Designed and fabricated a portable lap desk emphasizing comfort, usability, and durability. Selected a fixed-angle design to simplify manufacturing, created SolidWorks models and drawings for laser-cut birch components, and refined the assembly through sanding and fit adjustments to address interface tolerances.
